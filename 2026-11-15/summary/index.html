Grammar · Past Participle

  • past participle（过去分词）名字里有 past，但不一定表示过去。今天用到两种：
  • present perfect（现在完成时）：have / has + past participle，从过去开始，一直持续到现在，或者对现在有影响。
  • passive（被动语态）：be + past participle。
Examples

be → was / were → been

I have been learning English.

I finished my homework.（finished 是 past simple：过去某个时间做了这个动作）

My homework is finished.（finished 是 past participle：作业已经完成的状态）

Grammar · Active and Passive Voice

  • active voice（主动语态）：subject 是 doer（动作执行者），自己做这个动作。
  • passive voice（被动语态）：subject 是 receiver（动作接受者），接受这个动作。不关心是谁做的，或者想先说接受动作的人或物，就用 passive。
  • passive = be + past participle（be + done）。be 要跟着 subject 的单复数和时间变：现在用 am / is / are + done，过去用 was / were + done。
  • 想说出是谁做的，加 by + doer。
  • 判断步骤：先找 subject，问它是自己做，还是被做；被做就用 passive，再看时间和单复数选 be。
Examples from Class

The players claimed the field.（active）

The field was claimed by the players.（passive）

✗ The letter mailed by my uncle on Monday. ✓ The letter was mailed by my uncle on Monday.

✗ The gym were cleaned yesterday. ✓ The gym was cleaned yesterday.（gym 是单数）

✗ The starting players chose by the coach. ✓ The starting players were chosen by the coach.

The books are returned every Friday.（books 复数，every Friday 用 present simple）

The winners were announced by the principal yesterday.

  • 主动句改成被动句：Part A + active verb + Part B 变成 Part B + be + done + by Part A。
Active and Passive

The art club painted the posters. / The posters were painted by the art club.

The principal announced the winners. / The winners were announced by the principal.

My grandfather built this house. / This house was built by my grandfather.

  • 有些 verb 后面不能接 object（宾语），没有 passive，例如 disappear、die。
  • 科学事实、一直成立的事，用 present simple：Water covers most of the earth's surface. The sun rises in the east.
  • passive 用的是不规则动词表的第三列（past participle），要背熟：steal → stole → stolen；bring → brought → brought；sink → sank → sunk。
More Practice · Answers

1. are employed / employs

2. covers / is covered

3. was stolen / disappeared

4. died / were brought up（bring up = 抚养）

5. sank / was rescued

SAT · Active or Passive

  • SAT 的动词题：先判断 active 还是 passive，再看时间。
Question 1 · Kingdom of Benin

It ______ ruled by Oba Ewuare I from 1440 to 1473. 答案：was

from 1440 to 1473 是已经结束的过去时段，不用 has been；王国是被统治的，是 passive：was ruled。

Question 2 · Mackinac Bridge

Because the bridge ______ to withstand strong winds … 答案：was designed

bridge 不能自己设计自己，只能被设计；四个选项里只有 was designed 是 passive。

Punctuation · Though and Instead

  • 先删掉 modifier（修饰语）和插入成分，找出空格前后的核心句，判断是 IC（independent clause，独立句）还是 DC（dependent clause，从句）。
  • though、instead、however 是 conjunctive adverb（连接副词），不会把句子变成 DC。because、since、if、before、when 才会。
  • 空格前后都是 IC：只用 comma 一定错，要用 ; 或 .，或 comma + FANBOYS。
  • 连接副词放在哪一边：看它和哪两句形成对立或递进。它所在的那一句，是和前一句形成关系。A 和 B 对立，就放在 B 上；B 和 C 对立，就放在 C 上。
SAT Practice 10 · Question 3

Info A：Dr. Catherine Nakalembe gathers important data on crop health.

Info B：Nakalembe doesn't just compile the information, though;

Info C：she also shares her findings with African farmers.

对比在 A 和 B 之间（她不只是整理资料），though 属于 B，放在 semicolon 前：information, though; → A。

新词：compile 汇编、整理资料

SAT Practice 10 · Question 8

Researchers expected the sea otters to avoid the busy harbor, where boat traffic is constant; instead, the otters began resting near the docks.

where boat traffic is constant 是修饰 harbor 的 modifier，前半句整体是一个 IC。

后半句的 subject 是 the otters，verb 是 began；resting 不是 verb。

instead 说的是水獭实际做了什么，和前一句的预期相反，属于第二个 IC：; instead, → C。

Review Tips

  • 做动词题先问：subject 是自己做，还是被做？被做就用 be + past participle，再按时间和单复数选 be。
  • 继续背不规则动词表，第三列（past participle）要熟，passive 都用它。
  • 做 though / instead / however 题：先删修饰语找核心句，数 IC，再看连接副词和哪一句形成对立。
  • 做作业前先看一遍讲义和 Class Summary。
