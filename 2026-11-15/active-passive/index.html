Active and Passive Voice

GrammarPunctuation

Active or Passive?
Who does the action?

Voice

  • active voice（主动语态）：subject（主语）做动作，是doer（动作执行者）。
  • passive voice（被动语态）：subject接受动作，是receiver（动作接受者）；用be + past participle（过去分词）。

The whole verb group

  • tense（时态）和singular / plural（单数／复数）体现在完整verb group（动词词组）中：is cleaned / was cleaned / has been cleaned / will be cleaned。
  • regular verb（规则动词）的past form（过去式）和past participle同形，都是-ed；不能只看-ed判断。
  • doerThe players cleaned the field.
  • receiverThe field was cleaned by the players.
Test.
  1. 找subject和动作：subject做动作，还是接受动作？
  2. 接受动作 → 检查be + past participle。
  3. 根据时间和subject，检查完整verb group。
  • B1passive需要be + past participle；不能只留下past participle。
    • The letter mailed by my uncle on Monday.
    • The letter was mailed by my uncle on Monday.
  • B2先定voice（语态），再按时间和subject选verb group：passive 的 present simple 用 is / are + past participle，past simple 用 was / were + past participle。
    • The gym is cleaned every evening.
    • The gym were cleaned yesterday.
    • The gym was cleaned yesterday.
  • B3by + doer可说明谁做动作；subject仍是receiver。
    • The starting players chose by the coach.
    • The starting players were chosen by the coach.

Short Practice

1. Complete the sentence. Use the present simple.

Under the library’s current policy, the books ______ (return) every Friday.

Answer
are returned

2. Correct the mistake. Keep the winners as the subject.

The winners announced by the principal yesterday.

Answer
The winners were announced by the principal yesterday.

3. Complete the sentence. Use the past simple.

The art club ______ (paint) the posters for the game last week.

Answer
painted

SAT Questions

Question 1
Occupying a significant part of modern-day Nigeria, the Kingdom of Benin was one of the major powers in West Africa between the thirteenth and nineteenth centuries. It ______ ruled by Oba Ewuare I from 1440 to 1473.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

  1. is
  2. will be
  3. has been
  4. was
Worked Answer

D. was

  1. subject 是 It，指 the Kingdom of Benin。王国接受 rule 这个动作：被某位国王统治，所以是 passive。
  2. from 1440 to 1473 给出已经结束的过去时段；前句 was 也在叙述过去。
  3. 时间是 past，It 是 receiver → was ruled，选 D。
Question 2
The Mackinac Bridge, which connects the two peninsulas of Michigan, opened to traffic in 1957. Because the bridge ______ to withstand strong winds, its roadway can safely move several feet during a storm.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

  1. designed
  2. was designed
  3. designing
  4. has designed
Worked Answer

B. was designed

  1. Because 这个 dependent clause（从句）的 subject 是 the bridge；bridge 被人设计，是 receiver。
  2. Because 使这个 clause 不能单独成句，但它仍需要自己的 subject 和 finite verb（限定性动词）。
  3. 需要完整的 passive verb group：be + past participle；四项里只有 was designed 符合。
  4. was designed 可表达 1957 年通车前的设计，选 B。

More Practice · Active or Passive?

  • 先找 subject，判断它是 doer 还是 receiver，再按时间选 present simple 或 past simple。
  • 有些 verb 后面不能接 object（宾语），例如 disappear、die，它们没有 passive。

Present or Past, Active or Passive

1. Put the verb into the correct form: present or past, active or passive.

a. Two hundred people ______ (employ) by the company.

b. The company ______ (employ) 200 people.

Answer
a. are employed · b. employs
people 是 receiver（被雇用），用 passive；company 是 doer，用 active。

2. Put the verb into the correct form: present or past, active or passive.

a. Water ______ (cover) most of the earth’s surface.

b. How much of the earth’s surface ______ (cover) by water?

Answer
a. covers · b. is covered
科学事实用 present simple；a 句 water 是 doer，用 active；b 句 surface 是单数 receiver，用 is covered。

3. Put the verb into the correct form: present or past, active or passive.

a. While I was on holiday, my camera ______ (steal) from my hotel room.

b. While I was on holiday, my camera ______ (disappear) from my hotel room.

Answer
a. was stolen · b. disappeared
camera 被偷，用 passive：steal → stole → stolen；disappear 后面不能接 object（宾语），没有 passive，只能用 disappeared。

4. Put the verb into the correct form: present or past, active or passive.

a. Robert’s parents ______ (die) when he was very young.

b. Robert and his sister ______ (bring up) by their grandparents.

Answer
a. died · b. were brought up
die 是 verb，没有 passive（dead 是形容词）；Robert and his sister 是复数 receiver，过去发生，用 were brought up（bring up = 抚养）。

5. Put the verb into the correct form: present or past, active or passive.

a. The boat hit a rock and ______ (sink) quickly.

b. Fortunately everybody ______ (rescue).

Answer
a. sank · b. was rescued
sink（沉没）在这里没有 object，用 active：sink → sank → sunk；everybody 看作单数 receiver，用 was rescued。
