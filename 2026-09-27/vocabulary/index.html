Animals and Habitats

Grammar · Sentence CorePunctuation · Joining ClausesVocabulary · Animals and Habitats

1. Words

habitatn.

the natural home of a plant or animal · 栖息地

The wetland is a habitat for frogs, turtles, and many insects.

Word family: inhabit (v.) · inhabitant (n.)

Near-synonym: environment（更宽，指周围的一切条件）

organismn.

a living thing · 生物；有机体

Even the smallest organism in the pond needs clean water.

Word family: organic (adj.)

Near-synonym: living thing

adaptationn.

a change or feature that helps a living thing survive in its environment · 适应；适应性特征

Thick white fur is an adaptation that keeps arctic foxes warm.

Word family: adapt (v.) · adaptable (adj.)

predatorn.

an animal that hunts and eats other animals · 捕食者

The owl is a skilled predator that hunts mice at night.

Word family: predation (n.)

Meaning contrast: prey：被捕食的动物

preyn.

an animal that is hunted and eaten by another animal · 猎物

Small fish are easy prey for herons.

Word family: prey on (v.)：捕食

Meaning contrast: predator：捕食者

nocturnaladj.

active at night · 夜间活动的

Owls and bats are nocturnal, so they sleep during the day.

Word family: nocturnally (adv.)

Antonym: diurnal：白天活动的

endangeredadj.

in danger of disappearing completely · 濒危的

The sea turtle is an endangered species, so volunteers protect its nests.

Word family: endanger (v.)

Meaning contrast: extinct：已经灭绝的

contaminatev.

to make something dirty or harmful by adding poison or pollution · 污染

Chemicals from the factory contaminated the river.

Word family: contamination (n.) · contaminated (adj.)

Near-synonym: pollute

2. Practice

A Night at the Wetland

Choose the best word for each blank.

Every spring, a biology class visits a wetland near the school. The wetland is the natural (1) ______ of frogs, turtles, birds, and many kinds of insects. Every (2) ______ there, from the smallest insect to the largest bird, depends on clean water. The students arrive after sunset because many of the animals are (3) ______: they rest during the day and look for food after dark. A heron, a large (4) ______, walks slowly through the shallow water. The small fish near the surface are easy (5) ______ for this bird. One frog has green and brown skin that matches the leaves; this color is an (6) ______ that helps it hide. Last year, oil from a nearby road began to (7) ______ the water, and several fish died. The students also learned that one kind of turtle in the wetland is now (8) ______: only a few hundred are left in the whole state.
  1. (1) A. predator · B. habitat · C. adaptation
  2. (2) A. organism · B. offspring · C. habitat
  3. (3) A. endangered · B. contaminated · C. nocturnal
  4. (4) A. prey · B. predator · C. habitat
  5. (5) A. prey · B. predators · C. habitats
  6. (6) A. organism · B. offspring · C. adaptation
  7. (7) A. contaminate · B. conserve · C. protect
  8. (8) A. nocturnal · B. endangered · C. indigenous
Show answer
  1. B. habitat — “natural ___ of frogs …” = 天然的家。predator / adaptation 意思不通。
  2. A. organism — “from the smallest insect to the largest bird” 列举的是各种生物。offspring 指后代，habitat 不是 insect/bird。
  3. C. nocturnal — 冒号后解释：白天休息、天黑找食物。endangered 与冒号解释不符。
  4. B. predator — 下一句说小鱼是这只鸟的 prey → heron 是捕食者。
  5. A. prey — easy prey 固定搭配；鱼被鸟吃。
  6. C. adaptation — “this color … helps it hide” 是帮助生存的特征。三个选项都能接 an。
  7. A. contaminate — and several fish died → 油污染了水。conserve / protect 与结果矛盾。
  8. B. endangered — only a few hundred are left → 濒危。indigenous（本地原生的）与数量解释不符。

3. Use the Words

Write

Write three sentences about an animal you know. Use at least three words from the list. Underline the subject and circle the verb in each sentence.

Show example
  • Bats are nocturnal predators.
  • The forest is a habitat for many organisms.
  • Because oil contaminated the river, some fish are now endangered.
