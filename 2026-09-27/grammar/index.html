1. Find the Subject

  • Subject

    The students in the science club built a weather station.

    Show analysis

    The students [in the science club] built a weather station.

  • Head noun

    A box of old photos was on the shelf.

    Show analysis

    A box [of old photos] was on the shelf.

  • 先括介词短语

    The water in these two bottles comes from the river.

    Show analysis

    The water [in these two bottles] comes [from the river].

  • 状语放在句首

    After the storm, the students checked the sensors.

    Show analysis

    [After the storm,] the students checked the sensors.

  • 疑问句先改成陈述语序

    Did the students check the sensors?

    Show analysis

    Did the students check the sensors? → The students did check the sensors.

Model 1

Find the subject and the verb.

During the summer, a group of volunteers from the high school cleaned the river.
Show analysis
group → cleaned
  • [During the summer,] 是句首状语。
  • a group [of volunteers] [from the high school] 是完整主语；head noun 是 group。
  • cleaned 是谓语，the river 是宾语。

Practice 1

Find the subject and the verb.

People in snowy climates wear snowshoes for a variety of purposes.

snowshoe：雪鞋

Show answer
People → wear
  • in snowy climates 是介词短语，修饰 People。
  • snowshoes 是宾语（wear 什么？）。

Practice 2

Find the subject and the verb.

Every morning before school, the two brothers practice tennis.
Show answer
brothers → practice
  • Every morning before school 是句首状语（时间）。
  • practice 在这里是动词；tennis 是宾语。

Practice 3

Find the subject and the verb.

Do many guitarists prefer the lightest strings?
Show answer
guitarists → do … prefer
  • 改成陈述句：Many guitarists do prefer the lightest strings.
  • do 和 prefer 一起构成谓语。

Application 1 · SAT

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

After the printing press was introduced in 1440, handwritten manuscripts from Europe’s medieval period were often destroyed and the paper used for other purposes. In one instance, pages ______ a collection of Norse tales dating to 1270 were discovered lining a bishop’s miter (hat).

manuscript：手稿；Norse tales：北欧故事；miter：主教的帽子

  1. from:
  2. from,
  3. from
  4. from—
Show answer
C. from
  • 主干：pages … were discovered。
  • from a collection of Norse tales dating to 1270 是介词短语，是主语 pages 的一部分。
  • 介词 from 和它的宾语 a collection 之间不加任何标点。

2. Find the Complete Verb

  • Finite verb

    The sensor records the temperature.

    The sensor is recording the temperature.

    The sensor has recorded the temperature.

    Show analysis

    The sensor records the temperature.

    The sensor is recording the temperature.

    The sensor has recorded the temperature.

  • -ing 单独不能作谓语

    The sensor recording the temperature needs a new battery.

    Show analysis

    The sensor [recording the temperature] needs a new battery.

  • to + 动词不是谓语

    The team uses a sensor to record the temperature.

    Show analysis

    The team uses a sensor [to record the temperature].

  • having + 过去分词

    Having recorded the temperature, the team checked the results.

    Show analysis

    [Having recorded the temperature,] the team checked the results.

  • Compound predicate

    The students measured the plants and recorded the results.

    Show analysis

    The students measured the plants and recorded the results.

Model 2

Find the verb in each sentence. What is the job of “measuring”?

A. The students measuring the water wrote down the results. B. The students measured the water and wrote down the results.
Show analysis
A: wrote. B: measured and wrote.
  • A：measuring the water 修饰 students（哪些学生？）；谓语只有 wrote。
  • B：measured 和 wrote 是两个限定性动词，共用主语 students（compound predicate）。

Practice 4

Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb.

Look! The researcher ______ (examine) a leaf under the microscope.
Show answer
Look! The researcher is examining a leaf under the microscope.
  • Look! 表示此刻正在发生 → is examining。
  • 单独 examining 不能作谓语。

Practice 5

Correct the mistake.

The students collecting samples at the moment.
Show answer
The students are collecting samples at the moment.
  • 句中只有 collecting，没有限定性动词；补 are。

Practice 6

Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb.

Every week, the students measure the plants and ______ (record) the results in a notebook.
Show answer
Every week, the students measure the plants and record the results in a notebook.
  • and 连接两个谓语，共用主语 students；measure 是一般现在时，所以用 record。

Application 2 · SAT

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

In the late 1960s, inspired in part by the sight of laundry hanging on a clothesline, African American abstract painter Sam Gilliam began to create his iconic “Drape” paintings. He applied bold, saturated hues to large canvases and ______ them from ceilings or walls, causing the drooping fabric to cascade in dramatic loops and curves.

canvas：画布；suspend：悬挂；cascade：垂落

  1. to have suspended
  2. suspending
  3. to suspend
  4. suspended
Show answer
D. suspended
  • 主干：He applied … and suspended them …
  • and 连接两个共用主语 He 的限定性动词；applied 是过去时，所以 suspended 也用过去时。
  • causing … 是 -ing 短语，说明结果，不是谓语。

3. Separate the Core from Descriptions

  • 同位语

    Ms. Lee, our science teacher, keeps a turtle in the classroom.

    Show analysis

    Ms. Lee[, our science teacher,] keeps a turtle in the classroom.

  • -ing 短语修饰名词

    The researcher examining the leaves records their size.

    Show analysis

    The researcher [examining the leaves] records their size.

  • that 从句有自己的动词

    The plants that grow near the window need less water.

    Show analysis

    The plants [that grow near the window] need less water.

Model 3

Find the subject and the main verb.

The school garden, a project that students started last year, produces vegetables for the cafeteria.
Show analysis
garden → produces
  • [, a project that students started last year,] 是同位语，解释 garden。
  • started 属于同位语里的 that 从句，不是主句谓语。
  • 主干：The school garden produces vegetables.

Practice 7

Complete the sentence with the present-tense form of the verb.

The camera, a device that detects movement, ______ (send) an image every minute.
Show answer
The camera, a device that detects movement, sends an image every minute.
  • 括掉同位语 a device that detects movement；主语 camera 单数 → sends。
  • detects 属于 that 从句。

Practice 8

Correct the mistake.

The researcher studying local birds recording their calls every morning.
Show answer
The researcher studying local birds records their calls every morning.
  • studying local birds 修饰 researcher；句子缺主句谓语。
  • recording → records（every morning 表习惯）。

Application 3 · SAT

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

Botanists recognize over fifty different species of sunflower. One species, the silverleaf sunflower, ______ both an early-flowering ecotype that tends to grow in coastal areas and a late-flowering ecotype that grows inland.

botanist：植物学家；ecotype：生态型（同一物种适应不同环境的类型）；inland：内陆

  1. having included
  2. including
  3. to include
  4. includes
Show answer
D. includes
  • 括掉同位语 [, the silverleaf sunflower,] → One species ___ …：主语缺谓语。
  • 只有 includes 是限定性动词。
  • tends / grows 属于 that 从句，不能替主句提供谓语。
