Question 1 ✗ Incorrect

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

While many video game creators strive to make their graphics ever more ______ others look to the past, developing titles with visuals inspired by the “8-bit” games of the 1980s and 1990s. (The term “8-bit” refers to a console whose processor could only handle eight bits of data at once.)
  1. lifelike but
  2. lifelike
  3. lifelike, Correct answer
  4. lifelike, but Your choice

Your answer: D · Correct answer: C

Explanation · 解析
creators → strive；others → look。While 使第一部分成为 DC；others look to the past 是 IC。边界在 lifelike 后，故 DC, IC。 A: While 已引出 DC，but 不能再把它当成 IC 与下一句并列。 B: 前置 DC 结束处缺逗号。 C: lifelike 后逗号，DC, IC。 D: 虽有逗号，但 while + but 双重连接仍不成立。 先找从属标记；有主语和限定性动词仍可能是 DC。

Question 30 ✗ Incorrect

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

After immigrating from Mexico and obtaining U.S. citizenship, Octaviano Ambrosio Larrazolo entered politics, earning a reputation for being a fervent defender of Hispanic civil rights. In 1919 Larrazolo was elected governor of ______ in 1928 he became the nation’s first Hispanic U.S. Senator.
  1. New Mexico and
  2. New Mexico, Your choice
  3. New Mexico, and Correct answer
  4. New Mexico

Your answer: B · Correct answer: C

Explanation · 解析
Larrazolo → was elected；he → became。in 1928 是第二句时间背景，不是从属连接词。 A: and 前缺逗号。 B: 只有逗号，comma splice。 C: comma + and 连接两个 IC。 D: 两 IC 直接相接，run-on。 时间短语后的 he became 是完整主干。
