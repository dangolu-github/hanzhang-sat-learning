B15 ✗ Incorrect

There were all kinds of wooden toys.

Your answer: F
Correct answer: S · Sentence / 完整句
Explanation · 解析
there + be 表示“有……”；主语 all kinds of wooden toys 在 were 后，句子完整。

B16 ✗ Incorrect

That the mysterious man had built.

Your answer: S
Correct answer: F · Fragment / 句子片段
Explanation · 解析
that 引导定语从句，修饰上一句的 wooden toys；有主语和动词仍不能独立成句。
