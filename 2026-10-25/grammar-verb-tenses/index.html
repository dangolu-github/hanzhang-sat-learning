Verb Tenses and the Passive

GrammarPunctuation

Put the Time on the Verb
Tense

tense（时态）= 动词上的时间

  • finite verb（限定性动词）带 tense：它告诉我们这件事发生在什么时候；它的 subject（主语）就是做这件事或处于这个状态的人或物。
  • 一个 verb（动词）有 principal parts（四种基本形式）：base form（原形）practice、present participle（现在分词）practicing、past（过去式）practiced、past participle（过去分词）practiced。
  • 除了 present simple 和 past simple，其他 tense 都由 helping verb（助动词）+ 这四种形式之一组成（见下表）。
  • sentence 或 passage 里的 time words（时间词）决定用哪个 tense：yesterday、in 1881、since、by the time、now、every day。
  • Our team has practicedhas + past participle since August.
  • Our team practicedpast yesterday.
TenseFormExampleTime words
present simple（一般现在时）base form；第三人称单数 subject（he / she / it / our team）加 -sOur team practices after school.every day, usually, always
present continuous（现在进行时）am / is / are + -ingThe players are practicing now.now, right now, this week
past simple（一般过去时）past form（加 -ed，或不规则变化）The team practiced yesterday.yesterday, last week, in 1881
past continuous（过去进行时）was / were + -ingAt five o’clock, the players were practicing.at five yesterday, while, when
present perfect（现在完成时）has / have + past participleThe team has practiced since August.since, for, this season
past perfect（过去完成时）had + past participleBy then, the team had practiced twice.by the time, before, earlier
future simple（一般将来时）will + base formThe team will practice tomorrow.tomorrow, next week
Test.
  1. 找 finite verb（或空格）和它的 subject。
  2. 找 time words：这件事是现在、过去某个时候、过去到现在、还是过去更早？
  3. 按时间选 form：helping verb 定了，后面的形式就定了（上次课的规则）。
  • present simple：facts（事实）、habits（习惯）、现在仍然成立的事；第三人称单数 subject（he / she / it / our team）加 -s。
    • Water boils at 100 °C.
    • Our school starts at eight every day.
    • Yesterday our school starts at eight. (yesterday 是过去 → started)
  • past simple：过去某个时间做完的事；time words 常是 yesterday、last …、in 1881、when …。
    • The team practiced for two hours yesterday.
    • The school opened a new gym in 2019.
  • continuous（progressive，进行时）= be + -ing：那个时间正在进行的动作。
    • The players are practicing right now.
    • At five o’clock yesterday, the players were practicing.
    • The players practicing right now. (少了 are：-ing 前面要有 be)
  • present perfect = has / have + past participle：从过去开始、到现在还有关系的事；常带 since、for、this season。
    • Our team has won three games this season.
    • She has lived in Novi since 2020.
    • She has moved to Novi in 2020. (in 2020 是过去的一个时间点 → moved)
  • past perfect = had + past participle：两件过去的事里更早的那件；常带 by the time、before、earlier。
    • By the time the coach arrived, the players had finished warming up.
    • The team lost to a school that it had beaten easily the year before.
    • By the time the coach arrived, the players finished warming up. (arrived 之前已经做完 → had finished)

Short Practice

1. Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb.

Our team ______ (practice) for two hours last Saturday.

Answer
practiced

2. Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb.

Since August, the school band ______ (perform) at four games.

Answer
has performed（has been performing 也可以）

3. Correct the mistake.

When we reached the stadium, the game already started.

Answer
When we reached the stadium, the game had already started.（had started 也对）
