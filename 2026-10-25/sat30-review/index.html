Question 2 ✗ Incorrect

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

The African Games Co-production Market, one of over 180 annual international conferences supporting video game development, ______ the growth of the African gaming industry by helping start-up studios in Africa find partners.
  1. promote
  2. are promoting
  3. promotes Correct answer
  4. have promoted Your choice

Your answer: D · Correct answer: C

Explanation · 解析
subject 是 The African Games Co-production Market，一个 market，是 singular（单数）。 两个 comma（逗号） 之间的 one of over 180 annual international conferences … 是 noun phrase（名词短语） modifier（修饰成分），先拿掉；conferences 不是 subject。 promotes：singular verb，和 Market 一致。 promote：plural（复数） verb，和 conferences 一致了，不是和 subject 一致。 are promoting：are 是 plural，Market 不能用 are。 have promoted：have 是 plural；singular subject 要用 has。

Question 7 ✗ Incorrect

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

In 1903, Marie Curie shared the Nobel Prize in Physics with two other scientists ______ the first woman to win a Nobel Prize.
  1. and became Correct answer
  2. , and became Your choice
  3. and becoming
  4. and become

Your answer: B · Correct answer: A

Explanation · 解析
一个 subject Marie Curie，两个 verb：shared … and became …。and 后面没有新的 subject。 and became：became 是 finite verb，和 shared 一样是 past tense；and 前不加 comma。 , and became：and 后面不是 IC，不加 comma。 and becoming：becoming 不是 finite verb。 and become：和 shared 的形式不一致，要 past tense became。

Question 24 ✗ Incorrect

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

According to Naomi Nakayama of the University of Edinburgh, the reason seeds from a dying dandelion appear to float in the air while ______ is that their porous plumes enhance drag, allowing the seeds to stay airborne long enough for the wind to disperse them throughout the surrounding area.
  1. falling, Your choice
  2. falling:
  3. falling;
  4. falling Correct answer

Your answer: A · Correct answer: D

Explanation · 解析
先拿掉 According to … Edinburgh，找核心：subject the reason → verb is → that their porous plumes enhance drag …。 seeds from a dying dandelion appear to float in the air while falling 说明是什么 reason；falling 前面没有 be，不是这句的 verb。 falling：subject（the reason … falling）和 verb is 之间不加标点。 falling,：comma 把 subject 和 verb 分开了。 falling:：colon 把 subject 和 verb 分开了。 falling;：semicolon 前面只有 subject，不是 IC。
