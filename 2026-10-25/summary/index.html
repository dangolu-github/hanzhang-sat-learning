Homework Corrections

SAT Practice 30 · Question 2

The African Games Co-production Market, one of over 180 annual international conferences supporting video game development, promotes the growth of the African gaming industry by helping start-up studios in Africa find partners.
  • 先看空格前面有没有 verb（动词）：development 是 noun（名词），空格前没有 verb，空格要填 verb。
  • 两个 comma（逗号）之间的 one of over 180 … game development 是 appositive（同位语），解释前面的 noun，是非必要成分，可以删掉。
  • 删掉后找 subject（主语）的 head noun（核心词）：game、co-production、market 三个 noun 连在一起，最后的 market 是核心，前两个只说明是什么样的 market。
  • market 是 singular（单数），只有 promotes 能跟它；promote、are promoting、have promoted 都只能跟 plural（复数）subject。

SAT Practice 30 · Question 7

In 1903, Marie Curie shared the Nobel Prize in Physics with two other scientists and became the first woman to win a Nobel Prize.
  • subject 是 Marie Curie，verb 是 shared，object（宾语）是 the Nobel Prize；in Physics、with two other scientists 是 prepositional phrase（介词短语），先删掉。
  • 空格要第二个 verb：一个 subject 做两个动作。两个 verb 不能直接挨着，要用 and 连成一个整体。
  • and 连接的两个 verb 用同一个 tense：shared 是 past，所以是 became。
✗ Marie Curie shared the Nobel Prize became the first woman to win a Nobel Prize.
两个 verb 中间没有 and。
✗ Marie Curie shared the Nobel Prize with two other scientists, and became the first woman to win a Nobel Prize.
and 连的是同一个 subject 的两个 verb，前面不加 comma。

SAT Practice 30 · Question 24

According to Naomi Nakayama of the University of Edinburgh, the reason seeds from a dying dandelion appear to float in the air while falling is that their porous plumes enhance drag, allowing the seeds to stay airborne long enough for the wind to disperse them throughout the surrounding area.
  • 空格前的 to float 不是真正的 verb；appear 是 finite verb（限定性动词）。空格后还有 is，所以要看 clause（从句）结构。
  • 先删 According to …（phrase）和 from a dying dandelion（prepositional phrase）。
  • 主干是 The reason [seeds appear to float in the air while falling] is that …：中括号里说明是什么 reason；while falling 修饰 seeds appear to float。
  • the reason 是 subject，is 是 verb：subject 和 verb 之间不能用任何标点切开，所以 falling 后面不加标点。

Verb Tenses and the Passive

Only a Finite Verb Shows the Tense

  • tense 只体现在 finite verb 上。
  • nonfinite verb（非限定性动词）有三种：to do（infinitive，不定式）、doing、done。不管 subject 和时间怎么变，它们的形式都不变，所以不带 tense。
The seeds appear to float in the air.
The seeds appeared to float in the air.
appear 随时间变成 appeared；to float 不变。

Specific or Not Specific Time

  • present simple：永远成立的事实（truth）和习惯（every day、always）。
The sun rises in the east and sets in the west.
I brush my teeth every day.
  • present continuous：now、right now 正在做；也可以是现在这一段时间里正在做的事：this week、this year。
The players are practicing this week.
I am learning the piano this year.
  • past simple：具体的、已经结束的过去时间：yesterday、last Saturday、in 2020。
  • past continuous（过去进行时）：过去某个具体时刻正在进行：at five o’clock yesterday。
  • present perfect：since、for、this season 这类不具体的时间段，从过去一直连到现在，说的是对现在的影响。
The team practiced for two hours last Saturday.
last Saturday 是具体的、已经结束的时间。
✗ The team has practiced for two hours last Saturday.
last Saturday 是具体的过去时间，不能用 has practiced。
The players were practicing at five o’clock yesterday.
Our team has won three games this season.
this season = 这个赛季，还没有结束。
She has lived in Novi since 2020.
since 2020 = 自从 2020 年到现在。
✗ She has moved to Novi in 2020.
in 2020 是一个具体时间点，用 moved。

Present Perfect or Past Perfect

TenseLooks back fromMeaning
present perfect现在过去的事对现在的状态产生的影响
past perfect（过去完成时）过去的某一点过去的过去的事对过去的状态产生的影响
  • by then = 到那时为止（不是“那时候”）；by the time、before、earlier 也常带 past perfect。
The team has practiced since August.
从 8 月一直练到现在（10 月）。
By September, the team had practiced twice.
从 8 月到 9 月为止练了两次；不说现在。

Draw a Timeline

  • 两件过去的事：画一条时间线。先发生的那件用 past perfect（had + done），后发生的用 past simple。
When we reached the stadium, the game had already started.
比赛先开始（had started），我们后到（reached）。
✗ When we reached the stadium, the game already started.
看不出比赛是在我们到之前开始的。

Review Tips

  • 看到 verb 的空格，先找 time words（yesterday、since、this season、by the time），再选 tense。
  • 先问时间具体还是不具体：具体的过去（in 2020、last Saturday）用 past simple；从过去连到现在（since 2020、this season）用 present perfect。
  • 两件过去的事先画时间线，找出哪件更早。
  • 长句先删 prepositional phrase、appositive 这些 modifier（修饰语），再找 subject 的 head noun 和 finite verb。
  • 做 SAT 题不查词典：读不懂每个词，也能靠句子结构做对；每天背 SAT 词书的单词。
