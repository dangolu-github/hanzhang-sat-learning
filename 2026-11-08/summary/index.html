Grammar · Irregular Verbs and Present Continuous

  • irregular verb（不规则动词）的 past simple（一般过去式）和 past participle（过去分词）要成对记：base → past simple → past participle。
  • 今天先背 Group 1：be 到 drive。以后每节课提问，四周背完四组。
Examples from the Homework

pay → paid → paid（不是 payed）

go → went → gone

be → was / were → been

I finished my meal, paid the bill and left the restaurant.

  • at the moment = now（目前、当下）。看到这类词，动作正在进行，用 present continuous（现在进行时）：am / is / are + doing。
  • present continuous 的常见提示：now、right now、at the moment、currently，还有两个人正在对话的生活场景。
Examples

I'm trying to find a job at the moment. It's not easy.

Be quiet! The baby is sleeping right now.

Our class is currently reading a play by Shakespeare.

Punctuation · Joining Words

  • coordinating conjunction（并列连词，FANBOYS）：comma + FANBOYS 可以连接两个 IC。
  • subordinating conjunction（从属连词，because、although 等）：把 clause 变成 DC。DC 在前加 comma，IC 在前一般不加。
  • conjunctive adverb（连接副词，however、therefore、moreover、for example）：只表示关系，不能连接两个 IC，前后都要有标点。
Compare the Joining Words

The pool was closed, so we swam in the lake.（so：FANBOYS）

Because the pool was closed, we swam in the lake.（Because：DC 在前，加 comma）

We swam in the lake because the pool was closed.（IC 在前，不加 comma）

The pool was closed; therefore, we swam in the lake.（therefore：前面 ; 或 .）

  • which 引导的 relative clause（关系从句）修饰前面的名词，不能放在句号后面单独成句。
SAT Practice 4 · Question 4

✗ A local volunteer group restored the old greenhouse. Which building now serves as a classroom for gardening lessons.

✓ A local volunteer group restored the old greenhouse; the building now serves as a classroom for gardening lessons.

两边都是 IC：用 ; 或 .，或 comma + FANBOYS。

Punctuation · However and Therefore

  • Test：把 however 盖住，看剩下的是一个 IC 还是两个 IC。
  • 只有一个 IC：however 是 interrupter（插入成分），前后 comma 成对。
  • 有两个 IC：两个 IC 之间先放 . 或 ;，再看 however 属于哪一边。
  • however 只和它前面的那个 IC 形成 contrast（对比）。它放在第二个 IC 的开头或末尾都可以，意思一样。
One IC: However as an Interrupter

The referee, however, stayed calm.

The coach, however, was not worried.

Our team, therefore, needs a new goalkeeper.（Our team 是 noun phrase，不是 DC）

Two ICs: Where However Belongs

Many players were tired. However, the captain was not tired; he kept running.

Many players were tired. The captain was not tired, however; he kept running.

两句都对：however 都在 The captain was not tired 这个 IC 里，都是拿 captain 和 many players 对比。he kept running 是在补充说明，不是 contrast。

  • 做题先分 Info：Info 1、Info 2、Info 3，找出哪两个真正对立，however 就放进后面那个 Info 里。
Question 2 · Scala

Info 1：Scala 因为有 compiler，被叫作 compiled programming language。

Info 2：Compiling isn't exclusive to certain programming languages.

Info 3：any language can incorporate this tool.

Info 1 和 Info 2 对立；Info 3 是在 support（支持）Info 2。所以 however 留在 Info 2 末尾，后面用 ; 接 Info 3：languages, however; → D。

新词：compile 编译；exclusive to 只限于；incorporate 纳入、使用。
