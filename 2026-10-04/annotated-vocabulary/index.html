Animals and Habitats

Living Things and Their Homes

habitat / organism / adaptation

habitatn.

Meaningthe natural home of a plant or animal · 栖息地

Near-synonymenvironment（更宽，指周围的一切条件）

The wetland is a habitat for frogs, turtles, and many insects.

Familyinhabit (v.) · inhabitant (n.)

organismn.

Meaninga living thing · 生物；有机体

Near-synonymliving thing

Even the smallest organism in the pond needs clean water.

Familyorganic (adj.)

adaptationn.

Meaninga change or feature that helps a living thing survive in its environment · 适应；适应性特征

Thick white fur is an adaptation that keeps arctic foxes warm.

Familyadapt (v.) · adaptable (adj.)

Compare the meanings

organism 是生物本身；habitat 是它生活的地方；adaptation 是帮它在那里活下去的特征。
