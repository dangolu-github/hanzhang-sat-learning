How to hand in homework

  1. Open the homework to view the questions. 打开作业查看题目。
  2. Choose online answers or download the PDF to work on paper. 可直接网页作答，也可下载 PDF 打印完成。
  3. Hand it in one of three ways: answer online on the homework page, upload photos or a PDF, or send photos to your teacher on WeChat and press “I sent it on WeChat”. 三种方式任选一种即可，无需重复提交。
