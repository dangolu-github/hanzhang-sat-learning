Question 20 ✗ Incorrect

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

In modern plays, actors typically won’t acknowledge the ______ do so breaks the fourth wall, a metaphorical barrier between actors and audiences that allows viewers to suspend the knowledge that they’re watching a staged performance.
  1. audience. As to
  2. audience to
  3. audience. To Correct answer
  4. audience, to Your choice

Your answer: D · Correct answer: C

Explanation · 解析
左：actors → won’t acknowledge（IC）；右：To do so（不定式作主语）→ breaks（IC）。 A：As to do so breaks … 不成句。 B：run-on。 C：句号后 To do so breaks the fourth wall … 是完整句。 D：只用逗号，comma splice。 难点在主语是 To do so：先找动词 breaks，再问“什么 breaks？”。
