Build a Complete Verb Group
Verb group

verb phrase（动词组）

  • verb phrase = helping verb（助动词）+ main verb（主要动词）。
  • 第一个 verb 带 tense（时态），也和 subject（主语）一致：它是 finite verb（限定性动词）。
  • sentence 只有一个 verb 时，这个 verb 本身就是 finite verb。
  • 每个 independent clause（独立句）至少要有一个 finite verb。
  • Our teamsubject has been practicingverb phrase after school.
  • Has our team been practicing after school?
Test.
  1. 先找 subject：谁／什么？
  2. 从第一个 helping verb 圈到 main verb。
  3. 把第一个 verb 换成 past / present：能换，它就是 finite verb。
  • helping verb 决定后面那个 verb 的 form（形式）。
    • Our coach can explain the new rule.
    • The team has practiced every afternoon.
    • The players are practicing now.
    • The field was cleaned after the game.
  • -ing 前面没有 be，就不是完整的 verb phrase。
    • The players practicing after school yesterday.
    • The players were practicing after school yesterday.
    • The players practiced after school yesterday.
  • not / never 不属于 verb phrase。
    • Our team has never lost at home.
    • The new player did not miss a practice.
  • 一个 subject、两个 verb：and 后面的 verb 也要是 finite verb。
    • The captain checked the field and called the players.
    • The captain checked the field and calling the players.

Short Practice

1. Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb.

Our school band has ______ (perform) at three games this year.

Answer
performed
