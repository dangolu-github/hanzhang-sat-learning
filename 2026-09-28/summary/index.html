CLASS 01 · SAT READING & WRITING

Class Summary

Independent and Dependent Clauses

Review: Find Both Sentence Cores

  • 先把选项中的词完整放回原句，再找两边主语与完整谓语；不要只凭停顿或句子长度选标点。
The first map showed only the city center. The second map also included nearby villages.
map → showed | second map → included；两边都是 IC，不能只用逗号连接。
The first meeting attracted twelve readers. The club now meets every Friday afternoon.
meeting → attracted | club → meets；时态不同不影响两边都能独立成句。
Local residents donated the materials. Volunteers installed the sign during the summer.
residents → donated | volunteers → installed；volunteers 是后一句主语，不是 donated 的第二个宾语。

IC and DC

  • 有主语和谓语，不代表一定是 IC。because / although / when 等从属连词会使分句需要依附主句。
  • 分句通常围绕主语和限定性谓语构成；宾语不是每个分句的必备成分。例如 Birds fly. 也是完整句。
  • FANBOYS 是并列连词：for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so。because 不属于 FANBOYS。
  • 规范拼写：dependent clause；dirty；blurred；句中的 she 不大写。
I eat apples, and she eats apples.
IC, and IC.；两边能独立成句。
Because I do not like apples, I eat pears.
Because I do not like apples 是 DC；I eat pears 是 IC。because 已引出原因，不再加 so 重复连接。

DC First

  • 前置 DC 要连接后面的主句：DC, IC.
  • 不要用句号或分号把前置 DC 单独切开。although 已表示让步，不再用 but 重复连接主句。
Because the sensor failed, the team repeated the test.
Because 引导的原因从句在前，用逗号接主句。
Although the first result was unclear, the team kept the record.
Although … unclear 是 DC；the team kept the record 是 IC。
Because the glass was dirty, the image looked blurred.
不能写成 Because the glass was dirty. The image looked blurred.；前半句会成为 fragment。

IC First

  • 把紧密说明原因的 because 从句放在主句后面，通常直接连接：IC because DC.
  • 不要背成“所有后置 DC 都不加逗号”；本次规则针对紧密相连的原因从句，其他意义与结构仍需另行判断。
The team repeated the test because the sensor failed.
主句在前，because 从句紧接着说明原因。
The image looked blurred because the glass was dirty.
顺序改变，因果关系不变；这里不强加逗号；写完检查句末句号和逗号后的空格。

Keep the Whole Clause Together

  • 先看从属连词 → 找完整主语和谓语 → 判断 IC / DC → 选择标点。
  • Although 从句可能很长，包含比较或修饰信息；要找到它真正结束的位置，再接主句。
Although the newer material absorbed less water than the material used in earlier helmets, it offered the same level of protection during the laboratory tests.
Although … helmets 整体是 DC；it offered … 是 IC。逗号连接，句号、分号或再加 but 都不合适。
